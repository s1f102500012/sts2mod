[h1] Introduction [/h1]
This mod adds a Hextech Rune system inspired by League of Legends to Slay the Spire 2. It is a popular large-scale expansion mod for Slay the Spire 2.

There are currently 300+ Hextech Runes with a wide variety of effects. On each floor, you choose one random Hextech Rune, while monsters also randomly gain a Hextech of the same rarity — both sides then fight under ever-changing rules.

The mod also adds character-specific Hextechs, stat forgers, brand-new cards, and statistics feedback, giving every run a different build direction and challenge pacing.
